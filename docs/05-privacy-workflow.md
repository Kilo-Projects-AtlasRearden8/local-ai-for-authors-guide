# 5. Privacy-safe workflow

Running a local model means your words stay on your computer — but only if you keep them there. This section is a step-by-step workflow for writing a book privately, from first draft to finished manuscript.

## Principle: nothing leaves your machine

- The model file lives on your hard drive.
- Your prompts and your manuscript are processed locally.
- No internet is needed while you write or edit.
- No company reads your drafts.

## Step-by-step workflow

### 1. Set up a dedicated folder

Create one folder for your book, for example:

```
Documents/MyBook/
  drafts/
  notes/
  backups/
```

Keep everything related to the book in this folder. Don't scatter chapters across email, cloud drives, and messaging apps.

### 2. Work offline when you can

Once Ollama and your model are downloaded, you can disconnect from the internet entirely while writing and editing. The model runs entirely from your hard drive. Turning off Wi-Fi removes even the possibility of accidental uploads.

### 3. Keep local copies only

Save drafts as files on your computer (`.docx`, `.odt`, `.txt`, or `.md`). Avoid pasting your manuscript into websites, browser AI features, or apps that say they "sync to the cloud" unless you've confirmed they store data locally.

### 4. Back up to media you control

Copy your book folder regularly to:
- An external USB drive, or
- A second computer in your home

Label backups with the date: `MyBook-backup-2026-11-01`. Keep at least one copy separate from your main computer.

### 5. Version your drafts

Before big edits, save a copy:

```
Chapter3-draft1.docx
Chapter3-draft2-edited.docx
```

You don't need fancy software. Dating your files is enough to recover an earlier version if you change your mind.

### 6. Share safely

When you're ready to share a chapter with a beta reader or editor:
- Send the file directly (email, USB, or in person), or
- Use a private, password-protected method you control

Avoid pasting unpublished text into public forums, social media, or AI websites.

### 7. When you publish

This guide helps you write privately. Publishing (KDP, print, a website) is a separate step where you choose what to make public. Asdale Dynamics has separate beginner guides for KDP and book marketing if you'd like to explore that later.

## What this is not

This workflow protects your privacy while writing. It is not a substitute for legal advice on copyright, contracts, or publishing. If you have questions about rights or publication, talk to a qualified professional.

## Quick privacy checklist

- [ ] Model downloaded through Ollama (runs locally)
- [ ] Book folder on your computer, not only in the cloud
- [ ] Internet off while drafting and editing (optional but safer)
- [ ] Weekly backup to a USB drive or second computer
- [ ] Dated draft copies before major edits
- [ ] No manuscript text pasted into websites or public AI tools
