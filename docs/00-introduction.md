# 0. Introduction

## Who this guide is for

You want to write a book. Maybe you've already started, or maybe you're staring at a blank page. You've heard about AI, but you don't want to pay a monthly subscription, you don't want to upload your manuscript to a company's server, and you definitely don't want to become a programmer just to use a tool.

This guide is for you.

It's written for beginners — no coding required, no command-line wizardry, no fear. If you can download an app and click a button, you can run a local language model on your own computer.

It's also written with kindness. You don't need to be "good at tech." You just need curiosity and a little patience.

## What is a local LLM?

An LLM (large language model) is a program that reads and writes text. It can help you draft scenes, suggest dialogue, summarize chapters, catch repeated words, and rephrase clunky sentences — the kind of help a writing partner or editor might offer.

Most people use LLMs through a website: they type into a browser, and the text travels to a company's computer, gets processed, and comes back. That means your words leave your machine.

A **local** LLM runs entirely on your computer. The model file lives on your hard drive. Your prompts and your manuscript never leave your house. No internet connection is required after you download the model. No account. No subscription. No one reads your drafts but you.

## Why run locally?

- **Privacy.** Your unpublished manuscript stays on your computer. This matters whether you're writing a memoir, a novel, or a book about your work as a nurse.
- **Cost.** After you download a model, using it is free. Forever.
- **Availability.** Works on a plane, in a cabin, or anywhere without internet.
- **Control.** You pick the model. You decide when to upgrade. Nothing changes behind your back.

## What you need

- A computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux) made within roughly the last 6–8 years
- At least 8 GB of RAM (16 GB is more comfortable)
- A few gigabytes of free disk space per model
- An internet connection just for the initial download
- About an hour to set everything up the first time

You do **not** need a fancy gaming PC, a graphics card, or programming experience. A modest laptop can run small writing models just fine.

## What this guide covers

1. Choosing a model that fits your computer and your writing
2. Installing Ollama, the friendly app that runs local models
3. Prompts for drafting: chapters, scenes, dialogue, getting unstuck
4. Prompts for editing: line edits, copyediting, consistency, pacing
5. A privacy-safe workflow so your manuscript never leaves your machine
6. A printable quick-reference to keep by your keyboard

## A note on tone

This guide is written "like a friend would explain it." Plain English. Step by step. Never condescending. If something feels unclear, that's on the writing, not on you. Take it one step at a time.
