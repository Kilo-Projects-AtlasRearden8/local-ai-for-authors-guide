Welcome

You want to write a book. Maybe you've already started, or maybe you're staring at a blank page. You've heard about AI, but you don't want to pay a monthly subscription, you don't want to upload your manuscript to a company's server, and you definitely don't want to become a programmer just to use a tool.

This guide is for you. It shows you how to run a local large language model (LLM) entirely on your own computer — no cloud account, no API keys, no personal data leaves your machine.

Written like a friend would explain it. Plain English, step by step, never condescending. If something feels unclear, that's on the writing, not on you. Take it one step at a time.

How to use this guide

  1. Read the introduction to understand what local LLMs are and why they're private.
  2. Choose a model and install Ollama.
  3. Try the drafting prompts.
  4. Try the editing prompts.
  5. Set up your privacy-safe workflow.
  6. Print the quick-reference and keep it by your keyboard.
